## The golden rule: layers

Jilin winters (December–February) regularly sit between −15°C and −25°C, and the wind off the Songhua River makes it feel colder. Dress in three layers:

1. **Base layer** — thermal underwear, top and bottom
2. **Mid layer** — fleece or a down sweater
3. **Outer layer** — a windproof, waterproof down parka

## Don't forget

- Insulated, waterproof boots with good grip (icy sidewalks are the norm)
- A windproof face mask or balaclava — frostbite on exposed skin is a real risk
- Touchscreen-compatible gloves, plus a thicker pair for evenings
- Heat packs for pockets and boots
- Sunglasses — snow glare on sunny days is intense

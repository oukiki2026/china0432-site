## How to use this itinerary

Two days is the sweet spot for Jilin City: one day for the city itself, one for the spectacle. Pick your version: **winter (December–February)**, built around the rime ice, or **year-round** for the rest of the calendar. Day 1 is the same either way.

Think of it as a slow-travel weekend in a city the guidebooks forgot. The Chinese answer to two days in, say, Porto or Ljubljana: small enough to walk, strange enough to remember. Except here the strangeness is rime ice at −25°C and blood sausage for dinner.

New here? Start with the [Jilin City Travel Guide 2026](/travel/jilin-city-travel-guide/). Coming in winter? Read the [rime ice guide](/travel/rime-ice-guide/) and [packing list](/travel/winter-packing/) first.

## Day 1 — The city (any season)

**Afternoon: arrive & settle in.**
Arrive by high-speed rail at Jilin Railway Station or via Changchun Longjia Airport (see [how to get to Jilin](/travel/how-to-get-to-jilin/)). Check into your hotel (see [where to stay](/travel/where-to-stay-in-jilin/)) and drop your bags. If you land before noon, add a lazy riverside warm-up walk.

**Mid-afternoon: Jilin Confucian Temple （吉林文庙）, 1.5 to 2 hours.**
The Qing dynasty's first Confucian temple in the Northeast (1736; moved and expanded 1909). Walk the central axis in order (screen wall, Pan Pond, Lingxing Gate, Dacheng Hall, Chongsheng Hall) and don't miss the story: Manchu rulers building Confucius's temple at the empire's edge. Full guide: [Jilin Confucian Temple](/heritage/jilin-confucian-temple/).

**Evening: the riverfront & your first Jilin dinner.**
Walk **Songjiang Middle Road** along the Songhua River as the lights come on. This is Jilin's prettiest hour. Then dinner: order **guobaorou （锅包肉）** and **suan cai stewed pork** at a busy local restaurant. Ordering survival kit: [what to eat in Jilin](/taste/what-to-eat-in-jilin/).

**Night (optional):** Century Square's music fountain area for night views, then an early night. Day 2 starts brutally early in winter.

## Day 2 — Winter version (December–February)

**5:30 AM: depart for Rime Island （雾凇岛）.**
Yes, really. The rime is a dawn phenomenon. By 9–10 AM it's melting. Arrange a car the night before (hotel front desk or Didi), drive ~1 hour north to Wusong Island in Wulajie Manchu Town, and walk the frosted riverside as the sun hits it. Dress for −25°C: this is the coldest you'll be all trip. Everything you need: [rime ice guide](/travel/rime-ice-guide/).

**10:30 AM: back to the city, thaw out.**
Brunch somewhere warm. Noodle soup does the job. Nap if you need it; nobody will judge you.

**Afternoon: Beishan Park （北山公园）.**
Jilin's beloved hill park: temples, pavilions, and the best city panorama. Climb to the Picking Moon Pavilion, look back over the river valley, and warm up with the climb itself. In temple-fair season (around Lunar New Year) the park hosts Jilin's famous fair: crowds, snacks, folk performances.

**Evening: farewell feast.**
**Manchu hotpot** or the *sha zhu cai* "killing the pig" spread (white pork + blood sausage): the heaviest, happiest meal of the trip. You've earned it.

## Day 2 — Year-round version (March–November)

**Morning: Beishan Park （北山公园）.**
Same park, gentler weather. Temples, pavilions, morning tai chi, city views: Jilin's daily life at its most photogenic. Plan 2–3 hours at an easy pace.

**Midday: jianfen street & old-town lunch.**
Track down the **jianfen （煎粉）** snack street for pan-fried starch jelly with chili oil and vinegar (Jilin's signature street food, no Western equivalent), then graze: grilled skewers (*chuan'r*), cold noodles （冷面） in summer.

**Afternoon: riverfront & Songhua Lake option.**
Walk the Songjiang Middle Road riverbank in daylight, or take a half-day trip to **Songhua Lake （松花湖）** southeast of the city: boat rides, lakeside fish restaurants (order the lake fish stewed in an iron pot). Ask your hotel about current boat schedules; they vary by season.

**Evening:** riverside dinner, night walk, done.

## Extend it: the third day

- **Winter:** **Beidahu Ski Resort**, one of China's better ski mountains, about an hour from the city. Full guide: [Beidahu Ski Resort](/travel/beidahu-ski-guide/).
- **Any season:** **Wulajie Manchu Town** deep-dive: Manchu hotpot in its home setting, combined with Rime Island in winter.
- **Summer:** Songhua Lake deserves a full slow day, not a rushed afternoon.

## Practical notes

- **Book ahead:** rime-season hotels (Dec–Feb weekends) and your Rime Island car. Everything else can stay loose.
- **Getting around:** Didi (English interface) or hotel-arranged cars; the city center is walkable.
- **Cash & payment:** set up Alipay/WeChat Pay before arrival; carry some yuan as backup.
- **The one non-negotiable:** in winter, the 5:30 AM alarm. Sleep in and you miss the entire reason you came.

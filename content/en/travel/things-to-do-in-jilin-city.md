## Things to do in Jilin City in 30 seconds

- **The one thing you must not miss:** Rime ice on the Songhua River in winter. Jilin's rime is one of China's four famous meteorological wonders, and the city is the easiest place in the country to see it.
- **Winter:** [Rime Island](/travel/rime-island-jilin/) at dawn, [Beidahu skiing](/travel/beidahu-ski-guide/), and the winter festivals.
- **History and temples:** [Beishan Park](/heritage/beishan-park-temples/), where three religions share one mountain, and the [Longtan Mountain Fortress](/heritage/longtan-mountain-fortress/), a Goguryeo stronghold with 2.4 km of walls.
- **Museums and crafts:** The [meteorite museum](/heritage/jilin-meteorite-museum/) and [Manchu paper-cutting](/heritage/manchu-paper-cutting/).
- **Food:** Sauerkraut pork hotpot, dumplings, a proper Jilin breakfast, Korean food, and the night markets.
- **Budget:** Several of the best experiences cost nothing. See the free section below.

## Winter icons

**See the rime ice.** On cold, clear winter mornings, the trees along the Songhua River turn white with frost flowers. Our [rime ice guide](/travel/rime-ice-guide/) explains how rime forms and when your chances are best.

**Spend a day on Rime Island.** The island itself, about 40 km north of downtown in Wula Street, has two villages, a dawn walk along the riverbank, and a Manchu town behind it. It is a place, not just a viewpoint. Full plan in the [Rime Island guide](/travel/rime-island-jilin/).

**Ski Beidahu.** About an hour from the city, Beidahu is one of China's better ski mountains, with 870 m of vertical and a history as a 2007 Asian Winter Games venue. See the [Beidahu guide](/travel/beidahu-ski-guide/).

**Join the winter festivals.** The Jilin Rime Ice and Snow Festival runs through the winter with hundreds of events, from ice lanterns to snow sculpture. See the [winter festivals guide](/travel/jilin-winter-festivals/).

## Temples and old stones

**Beishan Park temples.** One mountain holds temples of three traditions: the Guandi Temple (1701), the Yaowang Temple (1738), and the Yuhuang Pavilion (1776). It is the densest piece of religious history in the city, and the park itself is a local morning ritual. Full story in the [Beishan temples guide](/heritage/beishan-park-temples/).

**Longtan Mountain Fortress.** A Goguryeo-era mountain fortress east of downtown, with roughly 2.4 km of rammed-earth walls along the ridgeline and national protection since 2006. The forest walk to the top is a pleasure in itself, and park entry is free. See the [Longtan guide](/heritage/longtan-mountain-fortress/).

**Confucian Temple and Sacred Heart Cathedral.** Two more layers of the city's history: a Confucian temple from the city's scholarly past and a Catholic cathedral from its treaty-port years. Both appear in the [city travel guide](/travel/jilin-city-travel-guide/).

## Museums and Manchu culture

**Meteorite Museum.** Jilin's meteorite fall is one of the largest witnessed stone meteorite showers on record, and the museum holds the famous Jilin No. 1 stone. See the [meteorite museum guide](/heritage/jilin-meteorite-museum/).

**Manchu roots and paper-cutting.** Jilin is a Manchu homeland city. The [Manchu roots guide](/heritage/manchu-roots-jilin/) covers the history, and the [paper-cutting guide](/heritage/manchu-paper-cutting/) covers the living craft: red paper, no sketch, bold symmetrical cuts rooted in Shamanic ritual.

## Free things to do in Jilin City

- **Walk the Songhua riverfront.** The city's best free show, especially on a rime morning or at sunset.
- **Climb Longtan Mountain.** Park entry is free, and the fortress walls and forest paths cost nothing.
- **Century Square in the evening.** The city's living room: dancing, skating in winter, and river views.
- **Browse the night markets.** Eating costs money, but wandering, watching, and photographing the stalls does not.
- **Morning in Beishan Park.** Join the locals for exercise, singing, and temple incense before the tour groups arrive.

For the full budget playbook, see [Jilin City on a budget](/travel/jilin-city-on-a-budget/).

## FAQ

**How many days do you need in Jilin City?**
Two full days cover the highlights: one day for rime or temples in town, one day for Beidahu or a day trip. See the [2-day itinerary](/travel/jilin-city-itinerary/).

**What is the best time to visit Jilin City?**
Winter, for rime ice and skiing. Autumn is second best, for red leaves around Jiaohe. See [when to visit](/travel/best-time-to-visit-jilin/).

**Is Jilin City worth visiting?**
If you like winter, folk culture, and cities without tourist crowds, yes. If you want big-city shopping and nightlife, it is not your city. The honest breakdown is in [who Jilin City is for](/travel/who-is-jilin-city-for/).

**What can you do in Jilin City for free?**
The riverfront walk, Longtan Mountain, Century Square evenings, and night market browsing all cost nothing. See the free section above.

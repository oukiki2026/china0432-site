## The essentials in 30 seconds

- **Where:** **Jiaohe**, a county-level city under Jilin's administration, about **1.5 hours east** of downtown Jilin City by car.
- **The two draws:** **Lafa Mountain** (886.2 m), famous for its **72 natural caves**, and the **Red Leaf Valley**, rated by Chinese National Geography as one of **China's top ten autumn sceneries**.
- **Best season:** **Mid-October** for the red leaves (roughly a two-to-three-week window). The caves and mountain are year-round.
- **Trip shape:** Leave Jilin City by 8 AM, caves in the morning, Red Leaf Valley in the afternoon, back by dinner. One full day, no overnight needed.

## Stop one: the mountain of 72 caves

Lafa Mountain has a local saying: **"eighty-one peaks, seventy-two caves."** The number is folk poetry, but the caves are real and they are the mountain's signature. The most famous are **Chuanxin Cave** (Piercing Heart Cave), **Taihe Cave**, **Yunguang Cave**, and the oddly named **Bubble Cave**, each with its own shape and legend.

The mountain itself is a three-sided pyramid, 886.2 meters at the summit, wrapped in primeval forest with **84.8% coverage**. The saying goes that no matter which angle you view it from, the mountain's profile never changes. Climbing to the top takes about two hours on maintained paths, and the summit gives a full panorama of the surrounding Changbai foothills.

If you know Kentucky's **Mammoth Cave**, you know vast underground systems. The cousin-not-twin part: Lafa's caves are smaller, scattered across the mountainside rather than one connected system, and half the experience is the forest walk between them. It is a mountain that happens to have caves, not a cave that happens to be under a mountain.

## Stop two: the Red Leaf Valley

Inside the same forest park, in Qingling Town, runs the **Red Leaf Valley**: a corridor of red, gold, and green stretching some **50 kilometers** along the mountain roads. Chinese National Geography put it on the **top ten autumn scenery** list, and in mid-October the hillsides look painted by hand.

The headline spots:

- **Valley within Valley (谷中谷):** the densest, most saturated stretch of red leaves. Walk in deep.
- **Qingling Waterfall:** a proper waterfall framed by red foliage.
- **Red Leaf Creek:** the stream mirrors the colors on both banks.
- **The glass skywalk:** a transparent viewing platform at 886 meters, red maples and sea of clouds in one frame.

**Timing is everything.** The peak is around **mid-October**, when over 90% of the leaves have turned, and the window lasts roughly **two to three weeks**. Miss it and you wait a year. Go on a clear weekday morning if you can; weekends in peak season bring tour buses and traffic on the mountain roads.

## The day trip plan

**8:00 AM:** Depart Jilin City. Drive east on the expressway toward Jiaohe (about 1.5 hours). Public transport exists (train to Jiaohe, then local bus), but a car or chartered driver makes the day dramatically easier.

**10:00 AM – 12:30 PM:** Lafa Mountain. Climb toward the caves, hitting Chuanxin and Taihe first. If you are short on time, the lower cave cluster plus the mid-mountain viewpoint is the efficient version.

**12:30 PM:** Lunch. Farmhouse restaurants near the park serve mountain food: wild mushrooms, river fish, corn cakes. Simple and good.

**1:30 PM – 4:30 PM:** Red Leaf Valley. Drive the corridor slowly, stop at Valley within Valley for the walk, Qingling Waterfall for photos, and the glass platform if heights do not bother you.

**5:00 PM:** Head back. You are in Jilin City by 6:30, in time for dinner. See our [food guide](/taste/what-to-eat-in-jilin/) for where the evening should go.

## Good to know

- **Autumn temperature swings are brutal.** Mornings near the mountain can be close to 0°C even when afternoons are mild. Pack a down layer, hat, and gloves in October.
- **Start early in peak season.** The red leaf roads clog by late morning on October weekends.
- **This pairs with:** our [day trips from Jilin](/travel/day-trips-from-jilin/) overview, and [best time to visit](/travel/best-time-to-visit-jilin/) for the seasonal logic.
- **Not just autumn:** the caves, the forest, and the mountain work in every season. Winter gives snow-covered caves; summer gives cool forest air. Only the red leaves are time-critical.

## FAQ

**Can I do this without a car?**
Yes, but it is slower. Take the train to Jiaohe, then the local shuttle to the park. Budget an extra hour each way versus driving. For a true day trip, chartering a car from Jilin City is the standard move.

**Is one day really enough?**
For the highlights, yes. Cave cluster in the morning, Red Leaf Valley in the afternoon, done. If you want to summit Lafa Mountain properly plus do the full valley walk, consider an overnight in Jiaohe.

**When exactly do the leaves peak?**
Mid-October is the safest bet, but it shifts with the year's weather. Local guesthouses and the park's own channels post the real-time color reports each autumn; check the week before you go.

**Is the glass skywalk scary?**
It is 886 meters up and fully transparent. If you are fine with heights, the view is spectacular. If not, the General's Sacrificial Altar viewpoint gives the panorama without the glass.

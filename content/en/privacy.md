## Analytics

We use tools such as Google Analytics to understand aggregate visits (page views, regions) and improve our content. This data is aggregated and contains no personally identifiable information.

## Cookies and advertising

We may display Google AdSense ads in the future. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your past visits. You can manage or opt out of personalized ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

## Affiliate links

Some articles contain affiliate links to travel booking platforms (for example, hotel or tour bookings). If you book through these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Articles that contain affiliate links always carry a disclosure notice. We only link to services we genuinely recommend in the article itself.

## Your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Contact details you share when emailing us are used only to reply to your message.

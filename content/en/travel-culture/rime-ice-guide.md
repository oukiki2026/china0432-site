## What is rime ice

Rime ice — known locally as *shugua* ("tree frost") — is Jilin's signature winter wonder. On nights when river mist meets temperatures around −20°C, the vapor freezes onto every branch along the bank, and by morning the whole riverside wears silver.

## When to go

- **Season**: late December to mid-February
- **Time of day**: around sunrise (roughly 7:00–9:00); the ice falls off once the sun climbs
- **Best conditions**: the bigger the overnight temperature swing and the thicker the river mist, the more spectacular the frost

## Where to see it

1. **Wusong Island** (Ula Street Manchu Town) — a river-encircled island with the densest rime ice
2. **Songjiang Middle Road riverbank** — right in the city, ideal for tight schedules
3. **Ashihada** — quieter, a favorite among photographers

## Tips

- Camera batteries drain fast in extreme cold; keep spares warm inside your coat
- Bring a tripod; backlit shots capture the ice at its most translucent
- The riverside wind is brutal — a windproof face mask and heat packs are essential
